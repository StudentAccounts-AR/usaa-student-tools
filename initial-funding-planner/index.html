02

Rating plan and milestones

Select the planned ratings and aircraft. The planner applies the fixed 2026 planning hours, pace schedule, and combined stage-check milestones.

!
Planning assumptions: Chief Pilot course weeks and FSP reservation targets guide the schedule. Stage checks and End-of-Course activity are included in the applicable stage milestone instead of priced separately. Training hours use the 2026 Academy Price Guide planning allowances, and AATD hours use the signed-syllabus creditable amounts. Commercial uses one selected aircraft for all 120 aircraft hours; instructed and solo time are included together.
04

Living Expense Payment Information

Enter the student information required to prepare Living Expense e-payments. This section is shown for loan-funded plans only.

Use the student’s current legal information. All address fields must describe a United States address and must not contain special characters.

United States only
E-payment timing: E-payments are processed by the end of the day on Monday of the week following submission. If a payment fails validation, a contact email will be sent to confirm the student’s personal details. Once those details are confirmed, the payment will be sent on the next business day.
